## Application rules
- Keep bilingual copy in typed content dictionaries and funnel sections in src/sections; language routes share the same page so composition stays consistent.
- Consultation submissions use a validated, rate-limited server function and service-only tables; visitors must never access stored medical or contact information.
- Generated imagery is illustrative, never evidence of a patient outcome or a real partner clinic.
- Keep marketing consent separate from consultation consent and never send contact or medical data to analytics.
